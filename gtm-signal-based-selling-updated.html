Executive Thesis
Move from isolated activity to coordinated, signal-driven revenue execution

We are not building a bigger sales tech stack. We are building a cross-functional signal-to-action revenue system.

Salesforce remains the operating system for AE, Solutions Consulting, and Customer Success work. Snowflake unifies product, customer, and commercial data. Claude/agentic workflows interpret signals, generate role-specific recommendations, and route actions through Salesforce tasks, Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Gong, Zendesk, Pendo, and SpotDraft only when there is a clear reason to act.

Team question

Who needs to act?

Prioritized AE, SC, and CSM actions based on buyer engagement, product usage, support risk, trial health, commercial readiness, and renewal timing.

Timing question

Why now?

Every action is tied to a named trigger: campaign engagement, demo request, trial usage, technical blocker, support escalation, renewal risk, expansion signal, or stage stall.

Execution question

What should we do?

Agent recommends the play, drafts the message or brief, routes to the right owner, coordinates the pod, and logs the outcome for learning.

Operating principle

Every workflow must answer five questions: What signal changed? Which role owns the next action? What context do they need? Which channel should execute it? Did it improve conversion, adoption, expansion, retention, or forecast quality? If a tool does not materially improve one of those answers, it is not strategic.

Cross-Functional 90-Day Roadmap
Stand up the signal operating model across AE, Solutions Consulting, and Customer Success before buying more tooling
Days 1-30

Define the operating model

Create shared Salesforce fields for Signal Type, Signal Source, Recommended Play, Action Owner, Accepted/Dismissed, Outcome, Trial Start/End, Success Criteria, Trial Health, Commercial Readiness, Demo Readiness, Technical Risk, Adoption Health, Support Risk, and Renewal Readiness. Establish AE, SC, and CSM ownership rules for each trigger.

Days 31-60

Launch role-specific pilots

Launch AE workflows for high-fit engagement, trial auditing, stalled opp recovery, and late-stage close planning. Launch SC workflows for demo readiness, technical evaluation planning, and demo follow-up. Launch CS workflows for onboarding activation, adoption drop, support escalation, and renewal readiness. Use Salesforce Flow first; add orchestration only if native flows are insufficient.

Days 61-90

Measure, govern, decide

Measure accepted actions, completion rates, conversion lift, demo quality, proof-plan completion, adoption recovery, renewal risk reduction, and expansion creation. Decide whether Outreach and Sales Nav are earning their spend. Evaluate Clay only against proven gaps: stakeholder change, contact gaps, closed-lost reactivation, and enrichment quality.
