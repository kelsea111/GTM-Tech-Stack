Executive Thesis
Move from more activity to better-timed, better-contextualized seller action

We are not building a bigger sales tech stack. We are building a signal-to-action revenue system.

Salesforce remains the operating system. Snowflake unifies customer, product, and commercial data. Claude/agentic workflows interpret the signal and generate recommended actions. Outreach, Gmail, Salesforce tasks, Slack, HubSpot, and CS workflows execute only when there is a clear reason to act.

Seller question

Who should I work?

Prioritized account and contact actions based on ICP fit, engagement, usage, relationship changes, and deal risk.

Timing question

Why now?

Every action is tied to a named trigger: usage spike, new stakeholder, competitor mention, campaign engagement, renewal risk, or stage stall.

Execution question

What should I do?

Agent recommends the play, drafts the message or brief, routes to the right owner, and logs the outcome for learning.

Operating principle

Every tool must answer one of four questions: Who should we focus on? Why now? What should we say or do? Did it work? If a tool does not materially improve one of those answers, it is not strategic.

90-Day Roadmap
Build high-confidence workflows first; do not buy more tooling until workflows prove the gap
Days 1-30

Instrument the signals

Define Salesforce fields for Signal Type, Signal Source, Recommended Play, Action Owner, Accepted/Dismissed, and Outcome. Pull Outreach and Sales Nav usage. Identify the first 10 named triggers.

Days 31-60

Build first workflows

Launch high-fit HubSpot engagement, usage spike expansion, renewal risk, competitor mention, and stalled opportunity recovery. Use Salesforce Flow first; add orchestration only if native flows are insufficient.

Days 61-90

Make vendor decisions

Decide whether Outreach and Sales Nav are earning their spend. Evaluate Clay only against concrete workflows: stakeholder change, contact gaps, closed-lost reactivation, and enrichment quality.
